Ceres is now available on Android

Ceres Assistant is now available as an app on Android. Install it on your phone, open it from your home screen and use it like any other app.

No need to look on Google Play. Ceres installs directly from ceres-assistant.com in Google Chrome with a single tap.

An AI assistant in your pocket

Ceres gives you a dedicated AI assistant that is always ready. Tap the icon on your home screen and Ceres opens instantly with your conversations and settings as you left them.

Write and code with AI on your phone

You can write, improve text, brainstorm ideas and work with code from your phone. On supported devices, Ceres can use Gemini Nano directly in Chrome for on-device AI assistance.

Works offline

After the first visit, the app saves what it needs on your device. The interface, conversations and settings are available offline, while cloud AI features need a connection.

How to install

  1. Open ceres-assistant.com in Google Chrome.
  2. Tap the Tap to install bar.
  3. Confirm when prompted.

The Ceres icon then appears on your home screen.

How to uninstall

Long-press the Ceres icon and select Uninstall, just like any other app.
