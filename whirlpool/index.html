CONSTRUCTION AGREEMENT

The Owner appoints the Contractor to carry out the construction and renovation of the property in accordance with the approved drawings. The agreement sets out the obligations of both parties. Materials and workmanship must meet the agreed specifications.

The agreed budget covers labour, materials and the transport of equipment to the site. The Contractor will submit an itemised quotation before work begins. Any additional cost requires the Owner’s written approval.

A deposit is payable upon signing. Further payments follow the completion of agreed stages, with invoices showing the work carried out and the remaining balance.

obligations cost budget deposit quotation
1 / 3

The Contractor must complete the works by the agreed deadline. An extension requires written approval. A penalty clause applies to delays attributable to the Contractor, with the amount and conditions set out in the agreed schedule.

Thermal insulation and waterproofing will be applied to the areas shown on the drawings. The Owner will select tiles, fittings and paint colours in time to avoid delays. Changes to these selections may affect material costs and the delivery schedule.

Any extra work requires written approval. The revised quotation sets out additional fees and payment terms. Both parties must accept the revised budget before work begins, and payment follows completion of the approved work.

deadline penalty materials payments approval
2 / 3

Upon completion, the parties will inspect the renovation together. Doors, windows and fittings must operate correctly, and any outstanding repairs will be recorded. The Contractor will remove waste and leave the property ready for use.

The final invoice will include approved expenses and deduct the deposit and earlier payments. Settlement of the outstanding balance follows acceptance of the work. Any refund or compensation agreed by the parties will be recorded separately.

The agreement specifies its expiry date and the warranty period. Contract termination for a material breach requires written notice and an opportunity to remedy it. Outstanding payments and any compensation are recorded separately, together with the final drawings and handover record.

expiry termination settlement compensation handover
